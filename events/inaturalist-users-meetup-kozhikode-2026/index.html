iNaturalist Users Meet Up Kozhikode 2026

Nov 7, 2026

🕊️ iNaturalist Users Meet Up Kozhikode 2026 brings together iNaturalist users and nature enthusiasts from Kozhikode and around Kerala for a day of talks, discussions, field exploration and a community bioblitz. Explore · Observe · Connect

📆 Date: Sunday, 8 November 2026

⏰ Time: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM

⛳ Venue: Kozhikode

👇🏻 Register here: https://forms.gle/F1EhN7brSYMVkLZt9

Registration fee: Rs. 50/-
GPay number: 9497136613 (the GPay QR code is shared at the end of the Google Form)

Agenda

🎤 Talk Sessions

  • Citizen Science: What It Is and Why It Matters — Speaker: Alen Alex
  • iNaturalist Observation & Licensing — Speaker: Manoj Karingamadathil
  • Exploring Underexplored Taxa: Opportunities and Challenges — Speaker: Navya S.

💬 Thematic Discussions

  • iNaturalist for the Conservation of RET Plants in Tropical Landscapes — Leads: Sanal C. Viswanath PhD & Harshid Pulparambil PhD
  • Citizen Science for the Effective Management of Biological Invasions — Leads: Unaisudheen T.P. & Aleem Yoosuf N.
  • Citizen Science Community in Kerala: Present & Future — Open discussion

🌱 Field Activities

  • Nature Walk & Bioblitz — Leads: Sneha C. & Siya Ul Haque

Program Highlights

  • Community Meet & Connect
  • Expert Mini-Talks (20 min)
  • Thematic Discussions (20 min)
  • Garden Visit & Nature Exploration
  • Community Bioblitz

Participant Perks

  • Community Building
  • Digital Memoirs & Special Badges
  • Certificate of Participation from the organising team
  • Knowledge & Experience Sharing
  • Food & Refreshments

Organisers
