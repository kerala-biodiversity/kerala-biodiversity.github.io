iNaturalist Community – Kottayam District Meetup at State of the Map Kerala 2026

Nov 12, 2026

🗺️ The iNaturalist Community – Kottayam District Meetup is happening as part of State of the Map Kerala 2026 – From Letters to Roads, the OpenStreetMap conference hosted at Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam. The meetup brings iNaturalist users, naturalists and open-knowledge communities together to talk about documenting Kerala’s biodiversity.

📆 Date: Sunday, 15 November 2026 (Day 2)

⏰ Session: Technical Session 2 – Track C, 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM

⛳ Venue: Mahatma Gandhi University, Athirampuzha, Kottayam

Speakers

  • Dr. B Sreekumar – Kottayam Nature Society
  • Mr. Tony Antony – Wikimedians of Kerala
  • Mr. Manoj Karingamadathil – Sahya Digital Conservation Foundation
  • Ms. Sneha C – iNaturalist Kerala Community
  • Mr. Alen Alex – Nature Conservation Foundation
  • Dr. Jinu Muraleedharan – Mushrooms of India Community

About State of the Map Kerala 2026

State of the Map Kerala 2026 is held on 14–15 November 2026, organised by OpenStreetMap Kerala and the Dr. R. Satheesh Centre for Remote Sensing and GIS, School of Environmental Sciences, Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam. iNaturalist Kerala Community and Sahya Digital Conservation Foundation are among its community partners and facilitators.

  • Full programme: State of the Map Kerala 2026 – Programs
  • Registration: State of the Map Kerala 2026 – Registration

Everyone is welcome to attend.
