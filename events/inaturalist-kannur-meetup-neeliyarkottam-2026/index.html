iNat Keralam Kannur Edition Meetup at Neeliyarkottam Sacred Grove

Nov 18, 2026

🌿 The “Walk With VC” community proudly organises the iNat Keralam Kannur Edition Meetup at the Neeliyarkottam Sacred Grove: a day of nature walking, biodiversity documentation on iNaturalist, and talks.

📆 Date: Saturday, 21 November 2026

⏰ Time: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM

⛳ Venue: Neeliyarkottam Sacred Grove, Mangattuparamba, Kannur (1.5 km west of Dharmashala junction on the Kannur–Taliparamba route)

Registration fee: Rs. 200/-

Neeliyarkottam Sacred Grove

Neeliyarkottam sacred grove, where “Neeli Bhagavathi” is worshipped and devotedly referred to as “Kottathamma”, is located at Mangattuparamba, 1.5 km west of Dharmashala junction on the Kannur–Taliparamba route. It extends over approximately 24 acres under private ownership, and the owners are supportive of research and conservation efforts there. It is a liana-rich sacred grove known for its rare wild orchids, and around 190 vascular plants have been recorded from the grove. The theyyam here is popular, as the devi’s entry from the vegetation at sunset is a divine scene for devotees.

About the Organisers: Walk With VC

“Walk With VC” is a Kerala-based community founded by the renowned environmentalist Sri. V. C. Balakrishnan, popularly known as “V. C.”. The community stands for the study, documentation and conservation of biodiversity. Under V. C.’s mentorship, the community, which has more than 150 members, carries out nature walks, camps, activities and challenges for children, awareness programmes and a popular webinar series.

It began in July 2022, when V. C. posted on Facebook about one of his casual visits to Shoolap Kavu, Cheemeni. Many from his circle wanted to join him on the next visit, and that nature walk became the starting point of the team.

Everyone is welcome to attend.
